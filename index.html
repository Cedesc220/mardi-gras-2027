Friday · Feb 5 · 5:30 – 8:30 PM

It's a Shucking Birthday Boil

Four Seasons New Orleans — outdoor space by Chemin à la Mer, Ground Floor

A birthday crawfish boil under the New Orleans sky. Come hungry, leave happy.

  • Crawfish boil with all the fixings
  • Outdoor setting by the river at Chemin à la Mer
  • Birthday celebration — details on the guest of honor coming soon
RSVP Venue
Saturday · Feb 6 · 11:30 AM – 1:30 PM

Bekah's Second An(nu)al Sausage Fest

Dat Dog — Magazine Street, Opa Terrace (covered outside space)

Come one, come all (okay, maybe not all) to the second annual sausage fest.

  • Dress code: Tracksuit Mafia (any tracksuit you'd like) or sausage-positive attire
  • Open bar + a buffet of culinary delights — assorted sausages, potatoes in various fried forms, allll of the toppings (including crawfish étoufée!)
  • Easy stroll after to watch the Krewe of Iris parade
  • Dogs welcome, as are well-behaved children

Unattended children will be given margaritas.

Classy as Shuck

Four Seasons New Orleans — dinner event

Dress to impress in New Orleans / Eccentric / Eclectic Cocktail and join the krewe for a Classy as Shuck dinner. Please RSVP ASAP — this is not an inexpensive party, so if you want the free food and drinks, be kind and reply.

  • Dress code: New Orleans / Eccentric / Eclectic Cocktail (see Bekah for attire questions)
  • Offspring permitted — just give a heads-up
  • Dietary restrictions accommodated with notice given prior to the start of Carnival season
