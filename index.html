Events

Friday, February 5 · 5:30 – 8:30 PM

It's a Shucking Birthday Boil

Four Seasons New Orleans — outdoor space by Chemin à la Mer, ground floor

A birthday crawfish boil under the New Orleans sky. Crawfish, corn, potatoes, and birthday cheer — come hungry, leave happy.

  • What: crawfish boil birthday celebration
  • Where: outdoor space by Chemin à la Mer, Four Seasons, ground floor
  • Dress: casual — it's a boil
Saturday, February 6 · 11:30 AM – 1:30 PM

Bekah's Second An(nu)al Sausage Fest

Dat Dog, Magazine Street — Opa Terrace, covered outside space

Come one, come all (okay, maybe not all) to the second annual sausage fest. Open bar and a buffet of culinary delights: assorted sausages, potatoes in various fried forms, allll of the toppings — including crawfish étoufée — and an easy stroll after to watch Iris.

  • Dress code: Tracksuit Mafia (any tracksuit you'd like) or sausage-positive attire
  • Open bar, sausage buffet, all the toppings
  • After: easy stroll to watch the Krewe of Iris parade
  • Dogs welcome, as are well-behaved children

Unattended children will be given margaritas.

Dat Dog
Saturday, February 6 · evening — 6 PM for now

Endymion Extravaganza

Caesars Superdome

Saturday is Endymion day — our krewe. The parade rolls at 4 PM through Mid-City and finishes inside the Caesars Superdome, where the ticketed Extravaganza party takes over for the night.

  • Time: 6 PM for now — to be confirmed once details firm up
  • Tickets: the Extravaganza is a ticketed event
